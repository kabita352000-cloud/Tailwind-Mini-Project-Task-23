Access for everyone

Greenwood is a place to slow down, discover and connect.

Our public library is more than a collection of books. It is a shared space where people can learn at their own pace, meet new people and find inspiration.

From quiet reading rooms to lively community events, Greenwood provides spaces for every kind of curiosity. Our collection includes fiction, children's books, science, history, art and digital resources.

We believe access to knowledge should be simple, welcoming and available to everyone. That is why membership is free and our public spaces are designed for the entire community.

Whether you are researching a new subject, looking for your next novel or simply need a peaceful place to work, Greenwood is open for you.
